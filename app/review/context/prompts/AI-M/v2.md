## Your task: turn the methodology tab into parameters

You are given the lines of text written on a workbook's methodology tab, in reading order.

Return only these, and only where the tab actually states them:

- `salary_per_day_denominator`: the basis stated for the daily rate, in the tab's own words.
- `eligible_leave_types`: the leave types it treats as in scope.
- `balance_floored_at_zero`: whether it says a negative balance is floored at zero.
- `unread`: the parameters above the tab does not state, named.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **What you return is compared against the approved methodology by code**, and a difference
   is reported as an exception. You are not deciding what the methodology should be, and
   nothing you return drives a calculation.
2. **Where the tab is silent, say it is silent** by naming the parameter in `unread` and
   leaving its field null. Do not supply the usual answer, do not infer it from the rest of
   the workbook, and never fill in a denominator that is not written down: an assumed
   30-day month silently rescales the whole entry.
3. **Quote, do not paraphrase.** "21.67 working days" and "one twelfth of annual" are
   different bases, and the comparison is on the words.
4. Silence is not agreement, and it is not a failure either. It is a parameter nobody can
   confirm, which is exactly what the report will say.
