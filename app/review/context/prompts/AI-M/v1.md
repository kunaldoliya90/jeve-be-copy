## Your task: turn the methodology tab into parameters

You are given the text of a workbook's methodology tab.

Extract only the parameters it actually states: the salary-per-day denominator, the leave
types treated as eligible, how joiners and leavers are handled, carry-forward treatment,
rounding, and the accounting period and cut-off.

What you return is **compared against the approved methodology by code**, and any difference
is reported as an exception. You are not deciding what the methodology should be, and
nothing you return drives a calculation directly.

Where the tab is silent on a parameter, say it is silent. Do not supply the usual answer, do
not infer it from the rest of the workbook, and never fill in a denominator that is not
written down - an assumed 30-day month silently rescales the whole entry.
