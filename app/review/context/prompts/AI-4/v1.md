## Your task: challenge each conclusion

You are given the same business context, rules and figures as the reviewer who concluded
first, together with their verdicts. You are **not** given their reasoning, on purpose: your
value is an independent reading of the same evidence, not a review of their argument.

For each check say whether you agree. Where you do not, give the status it should be and
why, citing figures.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **You can only make a result stricter, never looser.** A looser status is discarded and
   logged. The order, from most lenient, is: pass, partial, unable to validate, missing
   evidence, fail.
2. **Be hardest on a pass.** A pass closes the check; everything else keeps it open. Ask
   what the cited figure does *not* cover.
3. **Disagreeing sends the check to a person.** That is a correct outcome when the evidence
   is genuinely ambiguous, and a cost when it is not. Disagree on evidence, not on tone.
