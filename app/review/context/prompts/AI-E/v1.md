## Your task: does the evidence relate to this entry?

You are given the identity columns read off the posting tab - the entry actually booked -
and the same identity columns read off every other table and screenshot in the upload:
company code, company name, cost center, employee reference, account. You are not given
amounts, and you are not asked to check arithmetic.

Decide whether the supporting evidence is **about the same entity and transaction** as the
posting tab, not whether it is complete. A table missing an identity column entirely is
silent on this question, not a mismatch - evidence completeness is a different check, asked
elsewhere, and it is not yours to duplicate.

Return:

- `status` - `pass` when the evidence's identity values agree with the posting tab's, or
  disagree only in ways a reasonable preparer would explain (a subsidiary's cost center
  under the parent's company code, for instance). `fail` when the evidence is demonstrably
  for a different entity, company code, or population than the posting tab states. 
  `inconclusive` when there is not enough identity information anywhere to decide either way -
  never guess a pass to fill the gap.
- `rationale` - what you compared and why it settled the way it did, in one or two sentences
  a reviewer can act on.
- `mismatched_attributes` - every attribute that actually disagreed, each with what the
  posting tab said and what the evidence said. Empty on a pass.
- `citations` - which table and which value you relied on, worst evidence first.

Three rules specific to this step:

1. **A missing document is not a mismatch.** If a table's identity columns are absent, that
   table says nothing either way; do not count its absence as evidence of the wrong entity,
   and do not pass it as though it had agreed.
2. **A filename or sheet label proves nothing.** Decide from the identity values themselves,
   never from what a tab or evidence item happens to be called.
3. **When genuinely uncertain, say so.** `inconclusive` is a complete and useful answer. A
   guessed `pass` is not.
