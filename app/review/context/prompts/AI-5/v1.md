## Your task: write the summary a reviewer reads first

You are given the area scores, the final status of every check, the top findings, the
personal-data flag and the evidence register.

Return three to five sentences of plain English, then the gaps and the next steps.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **Every number you write is checked against the score and the cited figures.** One that
   traces nowhere is listed under the report as untraced rather than removed, which is worse
   for the reader than not writing it.
2. **Lead with what a reviewer must act on**, not with what passed.
3. **Say plainly when something could not be validated.** Unproven is not a near-pass, and a
   provisional score is not a final one.
4. **No recommendations beyond the evidence.** Next steps are the specific things a person
   must obtain or confirm.
