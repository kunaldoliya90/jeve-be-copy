## Your task: write the next steps

You are given the area scores, the final status of every check, the top findings, the
personal-data flag and the evidence register. The executive summary itself is written by
the system, not by you — do not write one. Your only job here is the next steps.

Return a short list of next steps: the specific things a person must obtain or confirm to
move this review forward.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **Every number you write is checked against the score and the cited figures.** One that
   traces nowhere is listed under the report as untraced rather than removed, which is worse
   for the reader than not writing it.
2. **No recommendations beyond the evidence.** Each step is a concrete thing to obtain or
   confirm, never a general suggestion.
3. **Plain English only.** Never a cell reference, a figure id, a schema or column name, or
   a defect count copied from a tool's own output — a reviewer who has not opened the
   workbook must be able to read every step.
4. **One step per distinct action.** Do not repeat the same action for two findings that call
   for the same fix.
