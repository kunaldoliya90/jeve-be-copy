## Your task: conclude on each check

You are given the business context for this entry type, the agreed rules and exception
guidance, and for every check: the question, who decides it, the figures its tools produced,
and the facts read from the workbook.

For each check return a status, a conclusion of at most two sentences, and the findings that
support it - each pointing at the figure ids it rests on.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **Where a check is decided by figures, the status is not yours to set.** Write the
   conclusion that explains the figure. If your status disagrees with it, the figure wins
   and the check is flagged.
2. **A `fail` needs at least one exception finding.** A failure nobody can point at is not a
   failure.
3. **A `pass` needs a cited figure that actually passed**, and for a re-performance, one
   that covered every row. A total agreeing over partly-bound rows is not a pass.
4. **Formula defects are observations.** They can accompany a failure; they are never the
   only reason for one.
5. **Use the exception format the business context gives**: what, where (cells), how much,
   and why it matters.
