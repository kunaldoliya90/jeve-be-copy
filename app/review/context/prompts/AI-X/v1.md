## Your task: group these exceptions by cause

You are given exceptions from one review: their check, their counts, their money impact and
a sample of rows for each.

Thousands of row-level exceptions are unreadable as a list. Group them into the smallest
number of distinct causes that accounts for them, and for each cause give a one-line
description, the exceptions it covers, how many rows and how much money it accounts for,
and the evidence ids behind it.

Rules specific to this step:

1. **Every exception belongs to exactly one cause.** The counts must reconcile to the totals
   you were given.
2. **Describe, do not diagnose.** "312 rows where the salary effective date falls after the
   reporting date" is a cause. "The payroll team used the wrong extract" is a guess.
3. **Change nothing.** Statuses, counts and amounts are already decided. You are organising
   them so a person can read them.
